Admin Guide: Team, Roles & Security

AI Work Orders · for company owners and admins · 5-minute read
The one thing to remember: everything about your team lives in one place — Settings → Team Members. Invite, change roles, reset a password, remove someone. You never need to contact support for any of it.

The four roles

RoleWhat they can doGive it to
OwnerEverything. Created the company. Can't be removed or demoted.The business owner
AdminEverything the owner can: team, settings, price book, imports, automations, the QuickBooks connection.Office manager, bookkeeper, a trusted #2
TechCreates and edits work orders on the job. Can't see settings or pricing setup.Field technicians, drivers, crew leads
ViewerRead-only — can look, can't change anything.Office staff who just need to look things up

Can invoice is a separate switch on top of the role. A Tech with "Can invoice" can push finished work orders to QuickBooks as invoices; without it, an admin does that step. Owners and Admins can always invoice.

Everyday tasks

🔑 Someone forgot their password

Two ways, both take under a minute:

  • You do it: Settings → Team Members → tap Reset password next to their name. They get an email with a link to choose a new password.
  • They do it: on the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter their email, and follow the emailed link.

Either way, nobody — not even you — ever sees anyone's password. A reset link is the only way to change one, and only the person with that email inbox can use it.

＋ A new employee starts

  1. Settings → Team Members → Invite Team Member.
  2. Enter their email, pick a role, tick "Can invoice" if they bill customers.
  3. Send them the app link. They tap Create an account and sign up with that exact email — they join your team automatically with the role you chose.

Each plan includes a set number of seats (Basic 5 · GPS 10 · Pro 20). Pending invites count toward it.

✕ Someone leaves

Settings → Team Members → tap ✕ next to their name. Their access ends immediately, even if they're signed in on their phone. Their past work orders stay in your records. If it was a mistake — or they come back — open Removed and tap Reactivate.

↕ Promote or demote

Change the role dropdown next to their name. It takes effect the next time they open the app.

Keeping the company secure

  • One login per person. Never share a password — invites are free within your seat limit, and shared logins mean you can't tell who did what.
  • Remove people the day they leave. It's one tap, and it's the single most important security habit.
  • Be stingy with "Can invoice." Only people who actually bill customers need it.
  • Keep a second Admin. If the Owner ever loses access, a second Admin can still run the company and reset passwords. Don't be a company of one admin.
  • Use a strong owner password. Eight characters minimum; a short phrase is easier to remember and harder to guess than a clever word.
  • QuickBooks is connected once, by an admin. Techs never touch it. If you ever suspect a problem, Settings → Disconnect QuickBooks cuts the link instantly, and reconnecting takes 30 seconds.

What's protected automatically

  • Every company's data is isolated from every other company's, enforced in the database itself.
  • Roles are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the app — a Tech can't invoice or change pricing even by tinkering.
  • A work order can only ever become one QuickBooks invoice, so a double-tap can't double-bill a customer.
  • QuickBooks tokens and API keys live only on the server, never in anyone's browser.
