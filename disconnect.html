You've disconnected AI Work Orders from QuickBooks Online

Your QuickBooks Online company is no longer connected to AI Work Orders.
Your work orders are safe. Disconnecting only removes the link to QuickBooks. Everything in your AI Work Orders account — work orders, customers, products, and pricing — is still there.

What changes now

  • New work orders can't be pushed to QuickBooks as invoices until you reconnect.
  • Products and customers won't sync from QuickBooks.
  • Invoices already created in QuickBooks are unaffected — they stay in your books exactly as they are.
  • Our access to your QuickBooks data has been revoked; the app can no longer read or write anything in your company.

Disconnected by mistake? Reconnecting takes 30 seconds

  1. Open AI Work Orders and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Under QuickBooks Online, click Connect to QuickBooks.
  4. Sign in to Intuit, choose your company, and approve. You're reconnected — your products and customers re-sync automatically.

Meant to disconnect?

Nothing else to do. You can keep using AI Work Orders for work orders on their own, and reconnect any time you like. If you'd also like your AI Work Orders account and data deleted, just email us and we'll take care of it.
